# PCOS Diet and Nutrition Guide

## The Role of Insulin Resistance

Insulin resistance is central to PCOS. Around **65-70% of women with PCOS** have insulin resistance, regardless of weight. High insulin drives the ovaries to produce more testosterone, worsening symptoms. Managing blood sugar is therefore a cornerstone of PCOS nutrition.

## Plate Method for PCOS Patients

Fill your plate using this visual guide at each meal:

- **½ plate**: Non-starchy vegetables — leafy greens, broccoli, cauliflower, peppers, zucchini, tomatoes
- **¼ plate**: Lean protein — chicken breast, fish, tofu, legumes, eggs, Greek yogurt
- **¼ plate**: Low-GI carbohydrates — quinoa, barley, oats, sweet potato, brown rice, whole-grain bread

This balances blood sugar, increases fiber, and helps with satiety.

## Foods to Emphasize

| Category | Examples |
|---|---|
| Whole grains | Steel-cut oats, quinoa, barley, buckwheat, brown rice |
| Legumes | Lentils, chickpeas, black beans, kidney beans |
| Lean protein | Chicken, turkey, fatty fish (salmon, sardines), eggs, tofu |
| Healthy fats | Olive oil, avocado, nuts, seeds, fatty fish |
| Vegetables | All — prioritize non-starchy varieties |
| Fruits (low GI) | Berries, apples, pears, citrus, kiwi |
| Anti-inflammatory spices | Turmeric, ginger, cinnamon, garlic |

### Cinnamon
May improve insulin sensitivity. Can be added to oatmeal, coffee, smoothies (½-1 tsp daily).

## Foods to Limit

| Category | Examples |
|---|---|
| Refined sugar | Sodas, candies, pastries, fruit juices |
| Refined carbs | White bread, white rice, instant noodles, crackers |
| Highly processed food | Fast food, packaged snacks, deli meats |
| Trans fats | Fried foods, margarine |
| Excess dairy (some people) | Monitor personal tolerance — full-fat often better than low-fat for satiety |

### Alcohol
Limit to 1 drink max per day. Alcohol raises blood sugar and triglycerides.

## Meal Timing

- Eat **3 balanced meals** plus 1-2 snacks if hungry
- Don't skip breakfast — a protein-rich breakfast (eggs, Greek yogurt) improves glucose stability
- Space meals 4-5 hours apart to allow insulin to fall between meals
- Avoid late-night heavy meals

## Sample Meal Ideas

**Breakfast**: Greek yogurt with berries, chia seeds, and a sprinkle of cinnamon
**Lunch**: Grilled chicken bowl with quinoa, roasted vegetables, and olive oil
**Snack**: Apple slices with almond butter
**Dinner**: Baked salmon with steamed broccoli and a side of lentils

## Hydration

- 2-3 liters of water daily
- Water before meals improves satiety
- Replace sugary drinks entirely

## Practical Tips

1. **Cook at home** more often — you control the ingredients
2. **Prep meals** in advance on weekends
3. **Read labels**: watch for hidden sugars (look for dextrose, maltose, high-fructose corn syrup)
4. **Don't be too restrictive** — extreme diets are not sustainable and can backfire
5. **Pair carbs with protein and fat** — this slows glucose absorption
6. Keep a **food journal** for 2 weeks to identify what triggers your symptoms

## Supplements Frequently Used in PCOS

| Supplement | Typical Daily Dose* | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Myo-inositol | 2000-4000mg | Improves ovulation & insulin sensitivity |
| D-chiro-inositol | 50-100mg | Usually used in 40:1 ratio with myo- |
| Vitamin D | 1000-4000 IU | Correct deficiencies first |
| Omega-3 (EPA/DHA) | 1000-2000mg | Reduces inflammation |
| Magnesium | 200-400mg | May improve sleep and mood |
| Chromium | 200-1000 mcg | Glucose metabolism support |

*Always discuss supplements with your healthcare provider — dosage is individual.

## Common Myths

❌ **"PCOS patients can't eat fruit"** — Low-GI fruits are fine in moderation.
❌ **"You must go keto"** — Ketogenic diets may help short-term but are not required; Mediterranean-style eating shows strong evidence.
❌ **"Skipping meals helps"** — Skipping meals worsens insulin spikes later.
❌ **"Dairy is forbidden"** — Evidence doesn't support this universally; monitor personal tolerance.

## Summary

Focus on whole foods, a Mediterranean-style anti-inflammatory pattern, regular meal timing, and gentle weight loss (5-10%) if overweight. Consistency beats perfection.
